    CONTRIBUTING.md asks for two habits that are easy to agree with and easy to forget: commit messages that describe the change, and diffs small enough to read. This lab turns both into something you can poke at — type a message and watch it pass or fail, then see what a format-on-save does to a seven-line change.

    01 · interactive

    Commit message linter

    Six checks, all of them lifted straight from the Commits section of CONTRIBUTING.md. Nothing is sent anywhere — the rules run in lab.js next to this page.

      Why A reviewer reads the subject line before anything else. "Add lab page for Ana" tells them what to expect; "updates" makes them open the diff to find out. Same work, different cost to everyone else.
